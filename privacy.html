Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Who uses this app

Job Apply Kit is a personal tool used only by its developer, Shanshuvo, with his own Google account.

Data accessed

The app accesses Google account data only through the permissions the developer grants it, and only to automate his own job application workflow.

How data is used

Data is processed on the developer's own machine or private CI environment. It is used solely to provide the app's features. It is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored as private secrets and are not published. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
